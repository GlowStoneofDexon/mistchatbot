# Mist Chat Bot 

Create a plan on building a telegram bot named, "Mist Chat Bot" or @MistChatBot



It's main objective is to talk with strangers without showing identiny. Meaning, an anonymous random chat bot. 



Medium: The chat is text, image, gifs/stickers, videos. 



The commands:

/search - 🔍Find a partner

/next - 🆕Stop current dialog and find a new partner

/stop - 🛑Stop current dialog

/help - 🆘How to use the bot

/vip - 💎Become a VIP

/link - 🔗Send Telegram profile URL to your partner

/myid - 🆔View your Telegram account ID

/paysupport - 💰Payment support

/rules - 📋Rules of the chat

/terms - 📖Terms and Conditions



Database: It will only store unique user id and related things to track a unique user and count their dislikes and reports. 



Logics:

When someone reports a partner, the whole conversation will be forwarded in "Partner 1" and "Partner 2" along with their ID-usernames to admin.



Every person can only chat with 15 people and if anyone wants to chat more, they will be paying a per month/week subscription. Or wait for 24 hours.





Rules:

If anyone is disliked equal or more than 65%, he will be stopped for 24 hours from that time. 



If someone is reported 10 times, he is banned. No access. 



If anyone is found selling illigal goods or any illigal activity (know from reports), they will be banned.
